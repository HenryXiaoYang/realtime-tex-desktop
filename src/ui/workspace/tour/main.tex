\section{Write on the pages}

Click anywhere in this sentence and type a few words. They go into the TeX file, and the page sets them as LaTeX will print them; Return starts a new paragraph.

\section{The source box}

Click this formula to see its TeX: $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$. The box shows the typeset result under it as you type. Click outside it, or press Escape, to close it.

\section{Formatting}

Double-click a word in this paragraph, then make it bold with the B on the ribbon. The ribbon writes the LaTeX for you, so you never type \texttt{\textbackslash textbf} yourself.

\input{chapter}

\section{When TeX breaks}

Delete the closing brace after the 2 in $\frac{1}{2}$. LaTeX can't typeset that, so the pages keep showing the document from before, and Problems in the status bar lists the error. Undo puts the brace back.

\section{Full runs}

Paragraphs update as you type. Page breaks, cross-references and the table of contents come from a full LaTeX run, which follows a moment later; Recompile runs one now.

\section{Export}

When you are done, Export PDF typesets the whole document into a PDF file.
